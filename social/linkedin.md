## 🚀 Option 1: Story & Feature Breakdown (Recommended for Maximum Engagement)

Cloud AI agents are great… until you see the surprise token bill, or realize your confidential code and PDFs just got uploaded to someone else's server. 🛑

Why can't an AI agent run like a real coworker directly on your own computer — 100% private, persistent, offline-capable, and completely free?

Today, I’m excited to introduce **OpenDot-Local v0.2.0**! 🤖✨

OpenDot-Local is an open-source native desktop app that runs autonomous AI agents ("Dots") locally on your hardware using **Ollama**.

Here is what makes v0.2 special:

🔹 **One-Click Native Installers (.exe & .dmg):** No complex Node.js or Docker setup. Install and run.
🔹 **First-Run Onboarding Wizard:** Automatically detects your local Ollama instance, scans installed models, and configures your local Edge/Chrome browser.
🔹 **Hardware Auto-Tuning:** Detects your GPU (NVIDIA, Apple Silicon, or CPU) and automatically assigns the optimal context window (4k, 8k, or 16k tokens) to prevent VRAM crashes.
🔹 **"Ask My Folder" Knowledge Engine:** Indexes local codebases or PDF folders using SQLite FTS5 + BM25 search, automatically excluding sensitive files (.env, keys) with verifiable citation badges.
🔹 **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** Connect your local agents to filesystem, GitHub, databases, and custom MCP tools with an encrypted local vault.
🔹 **Dot Packs (.opendot.json):** 1-click sharing, export, and import of agent personas, skills, and scheduled routines (includes bundled Research Assistant, Shopping Helper, and Code Reviewer packs).
🔹 **Privacy Ledger & Strict Offline Mode:** Complete auditability of network calls. Switch to Offline Mode to guarantee 0 outbound packets.

Everything runs on `localhost`. No API keys. No subscriptions. Nothing leaves your machine. 🔒
